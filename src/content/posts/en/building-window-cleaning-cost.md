Dull, dirty glass makes a building look neglected and hurts the image of the businesses inside. Building managers usually ask: **how much does building window cleaning cost**, and how often should it be done? Here is a guide.

## Building Window Cleaning Cost per m²

| Type of work | Market range per m² | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine cleaning (rope access) | IDR 15,000 – 25,000 | Buildings of ±5–20 floors, light to moderate dirt |
| Tall buildings / difficult access | IDR 25,000 – 50,000 | Extreme height, stepped or curved facades |
| Glass with mould / scale | IDR 50,000 – 80,000 | Needs special agents and repeated passes |

*General 2026 Greater Jakarta market ranges from various providers, not an official quote. The exact price is set after a survey.*

As an illustration, a building with 2,000 m² of glass at IDR 20,000/m² needs around IDR 40M per clean. Many providers offer better rates for regular contracts or large areas.

## What Affects the Cost

1. **Total area of glass and facade.** Large areas usually get a lower price per m².
2. **How dirty it is.** Ordinary dust is far easier than mould, hard-water scale, or cement residue.
3. **Height and facade shape.** Sloped, curved, or louvred facades take longer.
4. **Access method.** [Rope access](/en/what-is-rope-access/), gondola, or lift—each has different set-up costs.
5. **Work schedule.** Night or weekend work can affect the price.

## Comparing Cleaning Methods

- **Rope access:** quick to set up, flexible for complex facades, and needs no extra structure.
- **Gondola:** suitable when the building already has a permanent gondola.
- **Scaffolding:** rarely used just for cleaning because it is expensive and slow.

## How Often to Clean Building Glass

- **Every 3–6 months** for most city office buildings.
- **More often** for buildings near busy roads, industrial areas, or the coast.
- **Straight away** after renovation or construction work nearby.

Routine cleaning is also a chance to check the sealant. Cracked or detached sealant is one of the main [reasons building glass leaks](/en/why-building-glass-leaks/).

## What Building Window Cleaning Should Include

Before comparing prices, make sure the scope of work is the same:

- Cleaning the outside of the glass (and the inside if requested).
- Cleaning aluminium frames and trims.
- Treating special stains: hard-water scale, mould, cement, or paint.
- Cleaning ACP cladding, granite, or other facade panels.
- Setting up a safety zone below the work area.
- Reporting with before-and-after documentation.

## Tips for Choosing a Window Cleaning Provider

1. **Ask for a site survey** so the quote reflects real conditions, not guesses from photos.
2. **Ask about methods and cleaning agents** that are safe for the glass, frames, and glass coatings.
3. **Check technician competency** for work at height and their safety procedures.
4. **Agree a schedule** that doesn't disrupt occupants or tenants.
5. **Consider a regular contract** for better rates and a consistent schedule.

## Looking After Glass Between Cleans

- Fix gutters and drains that overflow onto the facade.
- Deal with sealant seepage early, before water stains set in.
- Avoid abrasive cleaners that can scratch glass or damage coatings.
- Note which areas get dirty fastest so they can be prioritised next time.
