Indonesia has a tropical climate: hot, humid, and rainy. House designs borrowed from four-season countries often feel stuffy and waste electricity here. The answer is the **modern tropical house**—a contemporary look designed specifically for our climate.

## Features of a Modern Tropical House

### 1. A roof with wide eaves
Eaves (overhangs) protect walls and windows from the sun's heat and driving rain.

### 2. Cross ventilation
Openings on opposite sides let air flow through the rooms, keeping the house cool.

### 3. Large openings and natural light
Wide windows and glass doors bring in light, shaded by eaves or screens.

### 4. A secondary skin
Breeze blocks (*roster*), timber screens, or perforated panels filter heat and add privacy while becoming a feature of the facade.

### 5. Natural materials
Wood, natural stone, and exposed brick feel warm and blend with the surroundings.

### 6. Integrated green space
A front garden, inner courtyard, or vertical garden lowers the surrounding temperature and freshens the house.

### 7. High ceilings
Taller rooms help hot air rise and escape through high openings.

## Materials That Suit a Tropical House

- **Roof:** tiles or roofing with heat insulation.
- **Walls:** brick or AAC block with weather-resistant exterior paint.
- **Facade:** concrete breeze blocks, wood-effect aluminium screens, or natural stone.
- **Floors:** granite, ceramic, or humidity-resistant parquet.

## Tips for Designing a Modern Tropical House

1. **Mind the orientation.** Minimise large openings facing the western sun.
2. **Plan airflow from the floor plan.** Cross ventilation works best when designed in from the start.
3. **Use good gutters and drainage** for heavy rainfall.
4. **Choose light exterior colours** to reduce heat absorption.
5. **Make room for green space**, however small the plot.

## Tropical Design on a Small Plot

On narrow urban plots, tropical principles still work: a void for vertical airflow, skylights, breeze blocks on the facade, and a small garden at the front or back. Pair it with a [minimalist interior](/en/minimalist-home-interior-design/).

## Modern Tropical vs Minimalist Houses

The two are often confused, but their focus differs. A minimalist house emphasises **simplicity of form and appearance**, while a tropical house emphasises **response to climate**—wide roofs, airflow, and protection from heat and rain. The best designs for Indonesia usually combine both: a clean minimalist look with tropical principles.

## Design Mistakes That Make a House Hot

1. **No eaves**, leaving walls and windows exposed to direct sun.
2. **Large west-facing glass** without shading.
3. **Fixed windows** that can't be opened, so air doesn't change.
4. **Ceilings that are too low** with no roof ventilation.
5. **Paving the entire plot** with no green space.

## Tropical Elements You Can Add During a Renovation

You don't have to build from scratch. Many tropical principles can be applied to an existing house:

- Add a canopy or eaves above the windows.
- Replace part of a wall with breeze blocks for airflow.
- Create a void or skylight in the middle of the house.
- Replace fixed windows with opening ones.
- Add a small garden or climbing plants for shade.
